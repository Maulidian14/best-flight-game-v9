EMBERLINE AIR

Meridian Archipelago Courier — fly the Kestrel-9, mind the fuel, land her gentle.

Take off: hold W to spool the engine, then pull ↑ once past 55 kt. Fly: ← → banks the wings and turns the nose; ↑ ↓ pitches. Keep speed above 47 kt or she stalls. Land: slow to 60 kt, flaps down (F), gear down (G), flare gently — a soft touchdown on the centreline scores. Fuel: burns with throttle; park on any strip to refuel. Damage: hard landings and hillsides hurt the hull. Mouse: hold and drag to steer. Camera: C switches chase / near.
A bush-pilot game for make.fun — original world, aircraft, art and sound. No network, no install.
